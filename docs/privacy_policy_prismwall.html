PrismWall Privacy Policy

Google Play Compliant 100% Safe & Private Effective: July 2026
1. Zero Login & Account-Free Architecture

PrismWall (Package ID: com.prismwall.app) is built on an account-free, privacy-first architecture. The application requires no account creation, no login, and no sign-up. We do not collect, process, transmit, or sell any personal identifying information (PII), such as your name, email address, phone number, password, biometric data, or contacts.

2. App-Private Isolated Storage Policy (Zero Gallery Access)

In strict adherence to Google Play Store User Data & Storage access policies, PrismWall operates with zero access to your device's shared photo gallery:

  • All downloaded wallpapers are saved strictly inside the application's isolated sandbox storage directory (getApplicationDocumentsDirectory()).
  • PrismWall does NOT request, declare, or utilize READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permissions.
  • Your public media library, photos, and personal documents remain completely untouched and isolated.
3. Third-Party Integrations & Network Transparency

PrismWall connects exclusively to one trusted external service:

  • Unsplash REST API: Used to search and retrieve curated, high-resolution aesthetic wallpapers and send mandatory download telemetry per the official Unsplash API Guidelines.

PrismWall does NOT integrate Firebase, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, ad tracking networks, or behavioral telemetry frameworks.

4. Storage Management & 100MB Automated Purge Cap

To preserve device health and avoid cluttering internal storage:

  • PrismWall enforces an automatic 100 MB local storage cap for cached wallpapers using a First-In, First-Out (FIFO) pruning algorithm.
  • Users can perform an immediate, complete wipe of all saved bookmarks, cache, and downloaded files at any time via Profile > Clear Cache & Local Data.
5. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA & GDPR-K)

PrismWall does not address or target individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Because our service collects zero personal information, we do not knowingly solicit or maintain data from children. If you believe a minor has interacted with the application inappropriately, please contact us immediately for assistance.

6. Security Standards

All communications with the Unsplash API are transmitted using modern TLS 1.3 encryption (HTTPS). Locally stored preferences (theme preference, favorites list) reside exclusively in Android's isolated SharedPreferences sandboxed container.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain ongoing compliance with Google Play Store policies and legal regulations. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
