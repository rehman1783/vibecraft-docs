1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Pro Calculator & Converter ("we", "our", or "the Application") handles user privacy. We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy.

🔒 Zero Data Collection Promise: Pro Calculator & Converter is a completely offline, standalone utility application. We do NOT collect, store, share, sell, or transmit any personal data.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Our core principle is user privacy by design:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for, collect, or store personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts.
  • No Usage Tracking: We do not track keystrokes, formulas, or how often you use specific features.
  • Local Calculation History: Your calculation history is saved solely on your local device. It is never uploaded to any cloud server or third-party database. You can clear this history anytime directly within the app.

3. Device Permissions

Pro Calculator & Converter requires minimal, non-invasive system permissions to function:

  • Vibrate / Haptic Feedback (VIBRATE): Used solely to provide tactile feedback when pressing calculator buttons. Can be controlled via device settings.
  • No Internet Permission Required: The application does not require internet connectivity to perform calculations or conversions.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: We do not request access to your Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, SMS, or External Media Files.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The Application does not include third-party advertising networks, third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), or behavioral trackers that profile users across applications.

5. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

The Application is designed for all audiences and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

6. Security

Because all calculations and unit conversions take place locally on your smartphone or device without internet transmission, your inputs remain secure within your personal device ecosystem.
